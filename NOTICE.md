# Provenance and reuse

The underlying document is a joint publication of the Government of Canada and
the Province of British Columbia:

> Canada–British Columbia Okanagan Basin Agreement (1974). *Technical Supplement V:
> The Limnology of the Major Okanagan Basin Lakes.* Office of the Study Director,
> Penticton, B.C.

Rights in the original report rest with the Crown. Nothing here asserts ownership
of it. This repository contains a **transcription** of its Appendix C tables plus
the code used to produce and verify that transcription.

- **Code** (`src/`): MIT.
- **Transcribed data** (`data/`): offered for reuse with attribution to the
  original report *and* to this transcription, so that errors introduced here are
  traceable to here and not to the 1974 authors.

Transcription errors are ours. Every value carries `raw_text` (the literal cell)
so any disputed number can be checked against the page image.
